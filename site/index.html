How it works.

  1. 01

    Start a SideQuest.

    Open it from the + menu in any conversation and drop the invite into the group chat.

  2. 02

    Scan the chat.

    Add a few screenshots. Text is recognized on your iPhone, and you choose which messages count.

  3. 03

    Everyone taps Done.

    Each friend adds their availability, budget and rough area. Planning waits for the whole group.

  4. 04

    Get three plans.

    AI suggests hangouts that fit everyone’s free time, lowest budget, age and location.

  5. 05

    Vote in Messages.

    Down, Maybe or Pass. The winner is picked fairly and deterministically.

  6. 06

    Lock it in.

    Share the winning plan to the thread and add it to your calendar in one tap.
