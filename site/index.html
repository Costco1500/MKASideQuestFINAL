How it works

Six taps from “we should hang out” to a plan on everyone’s calendar.

  1. 1

    Start a SideQuest

    Open SideQuest from the + menu in any conversation and drop the invite into the group chat.

  2. 2

    Scan the chat

    Add a few screenshots of the conversation. Text is recognized on your iPhone, and you choose exactly which messages count.

  3. 3

    Everyone taps Done

    Each friend adds their own availability, budget and rough area. Planning waits until the whole group is in.

  4. 4

    Get three plans

    AI suggests three hangouts that fit everyone’s free time, lowest budget, age and location.

  5. 5

    Vote in Messages

    The group votes Down, Maybe or Pass on each plan. The winner is picked fairly and deterministically.

  6. 6

    Lock it in

    Share the winning plan to the thread and add it to your calendar in one tap.
